Zuil Lamp

Elemental Table Lamp in Cast Mineral & Natural Wood

A cast mineral base, three pine dowels and a 3D-printed diffuser, held together by tension alone. No glue, no screws. Each base is poured and finished by hand in Amersfoort, in small batches, so no two are quite alike. The diffuser brings the colour, a playful hit against the quiet mineral and wood. It runs on USB, so it plugs into the charger you already own, and dims to the light you want.

Dimensions 17 × 17 × 41 cm
Weight approx. 1 kg
Power USB-A 5 V · 4.6 W
Made in Amersfoort, NL

Held together by balance

No glue. No screws.

The structure is assembled by hand, with no glue and no screws. The dowels push outward, the diffuser holds them in, and the two forces lock each other in place. Nothing is permanent, so every part can be repaired or replaced.

01. The Base

Cast, not molded

Every base is poured by hand from a mineral mix, then finished one at a time, small variations included.

02. The Dowels

Three, holding the light

Three pine dowels and not a single screw. They press outward against the diffuser, and that tension is what holds the lamp together.

03. The Diffuser

3D-printed

A playful hit of colour against the quiet mineral and wood.

Plugs into what you already have

Any USB-A port. No adapter in the box.

The strip ends in a standard USB-A plug, so Zuil runs from almost anything with a USB port: a phone charger, the TV, a laptop or monitor. Unplug it and take it outside on a power bank. A 10,000 mAh one keeps it lit for around six hours.
